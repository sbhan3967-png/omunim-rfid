🛡️ OMUNIM RFID Games

Two mini-games that show how RFID protects and counts a jewellery showroom.

🚨

Showroom Heist

A thief is pocketing jewellery and heading for the exit. The RFID gate flags unbilled tags — tap the thief before they escape! But don't accuse innocent shoppers.

📡

Tally Rush

Closing time! First tally the old way — barcode, walking to each tag one by one. Then feel the difference with RFID: stand still while one wave reads every tag at once.
